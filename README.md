# HalloweenLike

HalloweenLike is a *Space Invaders*-style game with a Halloween aesthetic! You play as a bat flying through the night, trying to survive against the creatures you encounter. To do so, you need to know the following controls:

- W: Move up
- S: Move down
- Click: Shoot "Halloween treats"

(You can also use P to debug collisions, and T to exit the current scene.)

It’s also important to understand how the monsters you’ll encounter behave! Here’s what you need to know:

- Ghost: Moves forward at a medium speed.
- Skull: Moves forward at a medium speed. It has a 50% chance each second to throw a bone, during which it will pause for 1 second.
- Witch: Moves forward at a slow speed. Each second, there is a 50% chance she will shift either up or down. She must be hit 3 times to be defeated.

I hope that with this information you can survive for as long as possible!
